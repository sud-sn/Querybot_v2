Precomputed copies

A copy keeps a metric already added up, by the fields you choose and by day or by month, on this server. Questions it holds exactly are answered from it, without a trip to the database: a dashboard opens at once, and the database is spared the same sums all day. Solviz makes it on the workspace's own connection, reading only, and makes it again on its schedule.

Solviz answers from a copy only when it gives exactly what the database would: the same rows, sums that add up again, whole days or months. Anything else (a field the copy does not keep, the rows left out by default, the last 90 days from a copy by month) is asked of the database as before. Each reader's table access and masking apply as always, and a reader with a row rule is always answered by the database. The answer says it was read from a copy and when the copy was made.
