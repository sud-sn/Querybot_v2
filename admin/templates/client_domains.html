Subject areas

An area names a set of tables and the words people use for them — sales, supply chain, finance. A question whose wording matches one area is answered inside it, so the planner, the retriever and the validator all see that area's tables and nothing else. A question that matches no area is answered exactly as it is today, so adding areas cannot make an un-grouped workspace worse.

When a second area scores close to the winner, the same question is also run there and the two answers compared. Agreement raises the answer's confidence; a disagreement is shown to the reader with both figures. That only happens when the runner-up is within {{ (decisive_margin * 100) | round | int }}% of the winner and both clear a score of {{ min_route_score }} — so ordinary questions are answered once.
