Row access

Decide which rows each person sees. A rule is written on a field (a store's region, a customer's segment, a doctor's ID) and narrows that table and every table linked to it: the sales of those stores, the orders of those customers. It holds in questions, dashboards, Home, scheduled emails, alerts and exports.

Rules

Rules on the same field are ways in: a person sees the rows any of theirs allows. Rules on different fields each apply. "Every row" lifts a field for the people it names. With no rule on a field, everyone sees all of it.
