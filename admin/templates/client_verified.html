Verified answers

An answer you checked is given again as checked. When a reader asks the same question, in the same words however they are written, Solviz answers it the way you verified, with no AI reading it afresh, on the data of the day they ask. The answer says who verified it. A question worded differently is read as usual.

Only an answer to a question asked on its own can be verified: not a follow-up, a suggestion clicked or an answer under a reader's standing condition. Each reader still sees only their own tables and rows: a reader without a table the verified answer counts from is told it is not available to them.
